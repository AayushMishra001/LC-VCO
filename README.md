5.8-GHz LC VCO Design in 180-nm CMOS | Cadence Virtuoso

• Designed and implemented a 5.8-GHz differential cross-coupled LC VCO in 180-nm CMOS with varactor-based frequency tuning, optimized LC tank, transistor sizing, and bias conditions for reliable oscillation.

• Implemented an LC-based tail-noise filtering technique to suppress tail-current-source noise and improve phase-noise performance while maintaining stable oscillation.

• Optimized DC power consumption, transistor sizing, and operating point by analyzing trade-offs among oscillation amplitude, frequency tuning range, phase noise, and power.

• Performed RF characterization of the LC tank using S/Z-parameter analysis; extracted complex impedance, equivalent resistance, and quality factor (Q) to evaluate resonator losses and guide passive optimization.

• Conducted transient, PSS, and phase-noise simulations in Cadence Virtuoso to verify startup, steady-state oscillation, output amplitude, oscillation frequency, and RF performance.

• Verified oscillation frequency and phase-noise performance across process, voltage, and temperature (PVT) corners and optimized the design for robust operation.

• Designed a symmetric/common-centroid RFIC layout with emphasis on device matching, differential symmetry, parasitic reduction, and RF-aware routing; performed PEX and post-layout re-simulation to evaluate parasitic impact on frequency, phase noise, and overall RF performance.
